    What I do

    I work in every area of Roblox Studio except sound. And I build the AI tools that speed that work up.

    1. 3D modeling and maps

      Pieces, props and full environments, built fast and finished clean.

    2. Lighting and VFX

      Light, atmosphere and effects that set the mood of every scene.

    3. Scripting
