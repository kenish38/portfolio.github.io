Briefing, blockout, art and optimization, always in that order.

Scale, style and technical limits are settled before the final art, so the map reaches the game without rework.

  1. Briefing

    I learn the experience, the audience and the references, and align scope, style and technical limits before any modeling.

  2. Blockout

    I block out the map in simple shapes to validate scale, paths and gameplay pacing right inside Roblox Studio.

  3. Art

    Final modeling, texturing, materials and lighting. This is where the world gets its atmosphere and its own visual identity.

  4. Optimization

    Technical review, asset organization and performance tuning so the map runs well even with many concurrent players.
