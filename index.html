    How it works
    
            
      
              
    • Hands on the real software
      Works directly in Blender, Substance Painter, Substance Designer, Photoshop and Roblox Studio. It models, textures, lights and assembles scenes instead of just generating images.
      • 
              
    • About 20 specialties
      Each with its own method, tuned to my taste, from modeling and materials to lighting, effects and high-resolution final images.
      • 
              
    • An AI jury
      10 specialized jurors. A blind one that sees only the image, a skeptic that checks nothing approved got worse, an advocate that tries to predict my reaction. The jury filters; I approve.
      • 
              
    • A memory that learns
      Every score and comment of mine becomes a lesson, and there are over a thousand. It predicts the score I will give, with a margin of error, calibrated on my history for each kind of work.
      • 
              
    • Orchestration
      An orchestrator oversees the working sessions, looks after the machine (memory and disk) and takes every problem to two independent AI analysts before deciding.
      • 
              
    • Handoff
      When an orchestrator reaches its limit, it writes the handoff for the next one. A blind reader checks that the text stands on its own before it counts.
      • 
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
            
    
              
    A real case: the system that fixes itself
    
              
      
                
    1. November 22
      A working session noticed on its own that it knew one of my rules and had not applied it: my scores rise over time, so an old approval does not protect an item.
      2. 
                
    2. It took the problem to the orchestrator.
      3. 
                
    3. Two AI analysts found the root cause: the rule was missing from the exact step that decides whether to reopen an item.
      4. 
                
    4. That same day, the fix went into the memory of every new session.
      5. 
              
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
            
    
              
    The engineering behind it
    
              
      
                
    • Protocol integration (MCP): the AI works inside each program
      • 
                
    • A panel jury with opposing roles, so nothing gets approved on autopilot
      • 
                
    • Score prediction with margin and drift: older scores weigh less because my bar keeps rising
      • 
                
    • Non-destructive work: the approved version is kept and the new one goes beside it
      • 
                
    • Two AI analysts from different models before every important decision
      • 
                
    • Continuity across sessions: the state is saved at every step, so nothing is lost
      • 
                
    • A safety lock: heavy jobs pause on their own when memory or disk reach the limit
      •
